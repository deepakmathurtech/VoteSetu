What happens when you click "cast"

  1. Your browser builds the ballot text: voter_id | candidate | timestamp
  2. Your browser-held non-exportable key signs locally (RSA-PSS / SHA-256) — the key itself never leaves this browser
  3. Only {voter_id, candidate, timestamp, signature} is sent to the server
  4. The server verifies the signature against your registered public key before accepting it
  5. Once mined into a block, you can fetch a Merkle receipt proving your vote's inclusion — without revealing anyone else's ballot
