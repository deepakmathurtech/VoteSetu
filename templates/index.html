How VoteSetu works

  1. Generate a signing key in your browser and register your public key
  2. Sign your ballot locally and cast it — the server never sees your key
  3. Signed votes are sealed into hash-linked, proof-of-work blocks
  4. Verify your own vote's inclusion with a Merkle proof, anytime
  5. Anyone can re-verify the entire ledger's integrity independently
